The lost-item owner and the finder must never communicate directly inside the app.

There must be:

No direct owner-to-finder chat
No exchange of private phone numbers
No exchange of personal addresses
No uncontrolled direct messaging
No pressure or negotiation between both parties

All communication must happen through:

Admin-controlled chat
Intermediary/drop-off centre chat
Controlled system notifications

This rule exists to reduce risk, conflict, fraud, harassment, and unsafe meetups.

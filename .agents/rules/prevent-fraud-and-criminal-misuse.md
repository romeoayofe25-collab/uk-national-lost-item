You must always consider how the app could be misused.

The system should protect against:

Fake lost-item claims
Fake found-item reports
Stolen goods being posted
Users trying to extort rewards
Users pretending to be owners
Users pretending to be finders
Repeated suspicious accounts
False identity verification
Abuse of chat systems
Unsafe meet-up attempts

The app must include reporting, monitoring, and admin review mechanisms.
